Privacy

Dice IQ is an offline puzzle. It does not create an account, collect personal data, or show ads, or use analytics. No data is collected.

Streaks, best scores, theme choice, Daily tries, the trial start date, the latest date the app has seen, and unlock status stay in on-device storage. Nothing is sent off the device.

The free trial starts when you tap Start 7-day free trial. That tap buys a free App Store non-consumable, and the trial counts from the original purchase date on the verified transaction. The same Apple ID keeps that date after a reinstall. The app also stores the date on this device so the trial works offline, and it keeps the latest date it has seen, so moving the clock backward does not add trial days.

The one-time unlock is a separate App Store non-consumable priced at $2.99. Dice IQ does not receive a payment card number. Restore Purchases runs when the app opens and when you tap it.
