Privacy

Twocurrent (Cozy Circuit Puzzles) is an offline puzzle. It does not create an account, collect personal data, show ads, or use analytics. No data is collected.

Stars, the sound setting, the trial start date, the latest date the app has seen, and unlock status stay in on-device storage. Nothing is sent off the device.

The free trial starts when you tap Start 7-day free trial. That tap buys a free App Store non-consumable, and the trial counts from the original purchase date on the verified transaction. The same Apple ID keeps that date after a reinstall. The app also stores the date on this device so the trial works offline, and it keeps the latest date it has seen, so moving the clock backward does not add trial days.

The Living Room (10 levels) stays free. The one-time unlock is a separate App Store non-consumable priced at $2.99. Twocurrent does not receive a payment card number. Restore Purchases is on the home screen and on the paywall. When the app opens, it reads the current App Store entitlements for this Apple ID.
