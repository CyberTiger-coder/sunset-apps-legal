Privacy

Sewvy is an offline app for learning sewing stitches. It does not create an account, collect personal data, show ads, or use analytics. No data is collected.

Favorites, practice progress, theme choice, the trial start date, the latest date the app has seen, and unlock status stay in on-device storage. Swatch photos you add stay on this phone. Nothing is sent off the device.

The free trial starts when you tap Start 7-day free trial. That tap buys a free App Store non-consumable, and the trial counts from the original purchase date on the verified transaction. The same Apple ID keeps that date after a reinstall. The app also stores the date on this device so the trial works offline, and it keeps the latest date it has seen, so moving the clock backward does not add trial days.

The one-time unlock is a separate App Store non-consumable priced at $4.99. Sewvy does not receive a payment card number. Restore Purchases runs when the app opens and when you tap it.

Camera and photo access are used only if you choose to add a practice swatch photo. The photo stays on this phone.
