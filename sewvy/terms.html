Sewvy is an app for learning sewing stitches in slow motion, on your own device. The stitch library works offline.

All techniques and lessons are free for 7 days after you start the free trial. After the trial, a preview of every technique stays visible, and the basics lessons, button repair guides, the stitch guide, the cheat sheet, and the glossary stay free. Every technique’s slow-motion player and steps then need the one-time unlock at the App Store price.

Restore Purchases returns a trial or unlock bought with the same Apple ID.

Needles, pins, irons, and sewing machines can injure. Children should sew with an adult nearby. The animations are illustrations, not exact fabric behavior. Test on a scrap first.
