Drawr Daily support

Drawr Daily is a daily drawing-lesson app from Sunset Apps. Lessons, timers, sketch photos, and in-app drawings stay on your device. There is no account. No lesson is free. A 7-day free trial opens every lesson. It does not charge you and does not renew. After that, Unlock Drawr Daily is $1.99 once. There is no subscription. Restore Purchases is on the purchase screen and in Settings.

Contact

Email sunset.apps.support@gmail.com. A postal address is on file with Apple and is available by email on request.

Photos and drawings

The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to photograph a sketch or pick a picture. Drawings you make in the app are saved in the same private gallery. Nothing is uploaded.

Drawing

On each lesson, choose Paper (draw, then take a photo) or In the app (draw with a fingertip or Apple Pencil). On iPad, the drawing sheet works in portrait and landscape.
