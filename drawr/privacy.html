Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026 · App: “Drawr Daily: 5-Min Lessons” (“Drawr” or “the app”) · Seller: Sunset Apps, based in California, USA (“I”, “me”) · Contact: sunset.apps.support@gmail.com · postal address on file with Apple, available by email on request

Drawr is designed to be private. In short: I do not collect any personal data.

What stays on your device

  • Your practice history (which lessons you did, dates, streaks, badges, and optional notes).
  • Photos of your sketches that you choose to take or pick, and drawings you make in the app. They are resized and stored inside the app on your device only.
  • Your settings (theme, appearance, timer, sound).

This information never leaves your device. I have no servers that receive it, and I can’t see it.

What I don’t do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No uploading of your photos or drawings.

Camera and photos

Drawr asks for camera or photo access only when you tap “Take photo” or “Choose,” so you can save a picture of your sketch. You can decline and still use every lesson.

Purchases

Starting the 7-day free trial, buying the $1.99 one-time unlock, and restoring purchases are handled by Apple on your device. The trial does not charge you and does not renew. Drawr does not receive your payment card details. The app does not send your name, email, photos, or drawings with that check. The trial start date is kept on this device so the trial works offline. There is no subscription.

Deleting your data

Settings → “Delete all my data” removes sessions, photos, and settings on this device. It does not reset the trial date or an unlock already recorded on this device. Uninstalling the app deletes the on-device copy. Restore purchases brings back the trial and the unlock for the same Apple ID. Device backups (iCloud) follow your own device settings.

Children

Drawr is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, email sunset.apps.support@gmail.com and I will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, under the CCPA/CPRA, or the EU/UK under the GDPR), you may have the right to know what personal information is held about you, and to access, correct, or delete it. Because I hold no personal information about you, there is usually nothing for me to access or delete, and your on-device data is under your control. I do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To make a request, email sunset.apps.support@gmail.com. I won’t treat you differently for using these rights.

Do Not Track

Drawr does no tracking across apps or websites, so it works the same whether or not your device sends a “Do Not Track” signal.

Security

Data on your device is protected by your device’s own security, such as your passcode. No method of storage is perfectly secure.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update the effective date above and note the change in the app’s release notes before the app collects any new kind of data.
