Privacy

Shapewright (Cut, Tab & Fold Paper Shapes) is an offline puzzle. It does not create an account, collect personal data, show ads, or use analytics. No data is collected.

Finished orders and their stars, stars spent on hints, hint progress, the sound setting, whether the intro has been seen, the trial start date, and unlock status stay in on-device storage. Nothing is sent off the device. The app does not ask for your name, location, contacts, or photos.

The free trial starts when you tap Start 7-day free trial. That tap buys a free App Store non-consumable, and the trial counts from the original purchase date on the verified transaction. The same Apple ID keeps that date after a reinstall. The app also stores the date on this device so the trial works offline.

The first 3 puzzles stay free. The one-time unlock is a separate App Store non-consumable priced at $2.99. Shapewright does not receive a payment card number. Restore Purchases runs when the app opens and when you tap it.
