Privacy

Shapewright (Cut, Tab & Fold Paper Shapes) is an offline puzzle. It does not create an account, collect personal data, show ads, or use analytics. No data is collected.

Finished orders and their stars, stars spent on hints, hint progress, the sound setting, whether the intro has been seen, the trial start date, the latest date the app has seen, and unlock status stay in on-device storage. Nothing is sent off the device. The app does not ask for your name, location, contacts, or photos.

The free trial starts on first play of puzzles 1, 2, or 3. Starting the trial buys a free App Store non-consumable, and the trial counts from the original purchase date on the verified transaction. The same Apple ID keeps that date after a reinstall. The app also stores the date on this device so the trial works offline, and it keeps the latest date it has seen, so moving the clock backward does not add trial days.

Puzzles 1–3 are free only during the 7-day trial. The trial does not open any other puzzle. The one-time unlock is a separate App Store non-consumable priced at $2.99, and it opens every puzzle, including after the trial. Shapewright does not receive a payment card number. Restore Purchases runs only when you tap that button. When the app opens, it reads the current App Store entitlements for this Apple ID. That is not a restore.
