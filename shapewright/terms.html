Shapewright (Cut, Tab & Fold Paper Shapes) is a paper-shape puzzle you play on your own device. Orders work offline.

The first 3 puzzles are free. A 7-day free trial opens the rest of the workshop. After the trial, a one-time unlock at $2.99 keeps every order. There is no subscription and there are no ads. Hints cost stars you earn in the workshop, not a separate purchase.

Purchases are handled by Apple. Restore Purchases returns a trial or unlock bought with the same Apple ID. The web build does not complete the App Store unlock. On that build, the free trial is recorded on this device.

The app is provided as-is. Governing law: the State of California.
