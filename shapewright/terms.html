Shapewright (Cut, Tab & Fold Paper Shapes) is a paper-shape puzzle you play on your own device. Orders work offline.

Nothing stays free. Puzzles 1, 2, and 3 are a free trial that lasts 7 days from first play. The trial does not open any other puzzle. When the 7 days end, those three lock as well. A one-time $2.99 unlock opens every puzzle. There is no subscription and there are no ads. Hints cost stars you earn in the workshop, not a separate purchase.

Purchases are handled by Apple. Restore Purchases returns a trial or unlock bought with the same Apple ID, and it runs only when you tap that button. The web build does not complete the App Store unlock. On that build, the free trial is recorded on this device.

The app is provided as-is. Governing law: the State of California.
